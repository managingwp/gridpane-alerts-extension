Keeps every alert from the GridPane notification bell in a searchable archive in your browser, and can POST each new alert to a webhook.

- [Install and first run](./getting-started/installation.md) — the Web Store or a release, plus the GridPane tab requirement.
- [Features](./reference/features.md) — everything the extension does today.
- [Roadmap](./reference/roadmap.md) — what is planned, possible and abandoned.
- [Collection and storage](./reference/collection-and-storage.md) — how alerts get in, where the copy can live, and the storage decision record.

:::note
This is an unofficial extension. GridPane does not publish or support it.
:::

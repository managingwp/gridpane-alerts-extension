Sync

Each check pages back through the newest alerts and the configured catch-up overlap (2 hours by default), so alerts GridPane adds late are still stored. Alerts are read from your signed-in GridPane session in this browser, and Chrome needs to be running for checks to happen. If GridPane rate-limits (HTTP 429), checks pause for 5, 15, 30, then 60 minutes.

Older history

Older alerts are collected automatically: after the newest ones, every check reads a batch of the pages below (20 per automatic check, more when you press the button). Fill older history only catches up sooner — one page of 8 every 1.5 seconds. If GridPane rate-limits it, the batch stops and the next check resumes from the same page.

Webhook

What gets sent

A POST with Content-Type: application/json, one request per alert, oldest first. Headers: X-GPAlerts-Event, X-GPAlerts-Id and, when a secret is set, X-GPAlerts-Signature: sha256=<hex HMAC of the raw body>. Failed deliveries are queued and retried with backoff (2, 4, 8… up to 60 minutes).
